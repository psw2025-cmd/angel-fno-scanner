# Decision: D-06 — PREMARKET_VS_ACTUAL 50% Opening Gap Direction Accuracy

**Defect ID:** D-06  
**Found Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Severity:** HIGH  
**Status:** OPEN  

---

## 1. Description
Audit of the `PREMARKET_VS_ACTUAL` sheet tab revealed that out of 6 tracked symbols, only 3 correctly predicted opening gap direction (50% hit rate, equivalent to random chance). The mean gap error was 2.54 percentage points.

## 2. Evidence
- Export from `PREMARKET_VS_ACTUAL` (historical session 2026-09-28):
  - Total evaluated symbols: 6
  - Correct direction predictions: 3
  - Incorrect direction predictions: 3
  - Mean Absolute Error (MAE): 2.54%
- The multi-factor scoring model currently weights overnight global sentiment and option Greeks without adequate sector-specific momentum calibration.

## 3. Impact
- Low forward prediction confidence for Target A (underlying pre-open gap).
- High risk of adverse selection if paper trades rely solely on uncalibrated opening gap direction.

## 4. Options Considered
1. **Option A (Recommended):** Conduct a formal model review and calibration session using chronological historical data. Do not adjust model weights ad-hoc in production.
2. **Option B:** Expand sample size to all 219 underlyings before evaluating accuracy. (Recommended as an evaluation enhancement).
3. **Option C:** Disable pre-market gap trades until hit rate exceeds 65% on untouched forward test data.

## 5. Recommended Action
1. Keep Target A (opening gap) separate from Target B/C (CE/PE contract ranking) per `AGENTS.md` Section 16.
2. Maintain append-only outcome ledger in BigQuery `next_day_gap_predictions`.
3. Require at least 30 forward sessions of evidence before promoting candidate weight adjustments.

## 6. Open Questions
- Is 6 symbols an artifact of filtering by high conviction (>75%), or was data missing for the remainder of the universe?
- Should opening gap predictions require news catalyst confirmation before being journaled?
